# Posting guidelines

These rules apply to every post, written by hand or by the bot.

**Voice.** Plain and human. No em-dashes or en-dashes. No "In a significant move", "game-changer", "unlock", or stacked fragments. Say what happened, then why it matters.

**Facts.** A number or name appears only if it is in the source text or on our own site. Current-affairs notes are grounded in the article text and always link to the source. When the text is too thin, the bot skips the story.

**Promotion.** At most one promotional post a day, never two in a row. State the real price and the real refund terms. No countdowns, no "last chance", no invented offers. A Telegram offer, if we run one, has a fixed discount and a real end date.

**Volume.** Daily current affairs at 07:00 IST, then up to three posts: a tip around 12:30, a product or blog around 17:30, a data fact around 20:30. Nothing between 22:00 and 07:00.

**Links.** Every link carries `utm_source=telegram` so the site can show which posts bring readers.

**Sources.** PIB, The Hindu and Indian Express feeds, summarised in our own words with a link back. We do not copy article text.
